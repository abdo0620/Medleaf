INDICATIONS AND USAGE:
Uses Anti-acne Tighten skin Moisturize Improve fine lines Whitening

WARNINGS:
Warning For external use only.

DO NOT USE:
Do not use Do not use on damaged or broken skin. Avoid contact with eyes when using this product. Rinse with water to remove.

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:
Directions Apply thin layer on acne-prone/oily areas after cleansing. Use 1–2 times daily, preferably at night; sunscreen during day. Start every other day; increase frequency if tolerated. Reduce use or add moisturizer if dryness/peeling occurs.

STOP USE IF:
Stop use and ask a doctor if • The condition worsens or does not improve after 7 days of use. • Excessive skin irritation, redness, burning, or peeling occurs. • Signs of allergic reaction develop, such as rash, itching, or swelling. • The product is accidentally ingested.
