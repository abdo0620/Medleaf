DRUG NAME: Leader Earwax Removal Drops Earwax Removal Aid
GENERIC NAME: CARBAMIDE PEROXIDE
MANUFACTURER: Cardinal Health, Inc.
ROUTE: TOPICAL

ACTIVE INGREDIENT:
Active ingredient Carbamide peroxide 6.5% Purpose Earwax removal aid

INACTIVE INGREDIENTS:
Inactive ingredients anhydrous citric acid, glycerin, propylene glycol, sodium citrate, sodium lauryl sulfate, water

INDICATIONS AND USAGE:
Uses for occasional use as an aid to soften, loosen, and remove excessive earwax

WARNINGS:
Warnings For use in the ear only Ask a doctor before use if you have ear drainage or discharge ear pain irritation, or rash in the ear dizziness an injury or perforation (hole) of the ear drum recently had ear surgery When using this product avoid contact with the eyes Stop use and ask a doctor if you need to use for more than 4 days excessive earwax remains after use of this product Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center (1-800-222-1222) right away.

ASK A DOCTOR BEFORE USE:
Ask a doctor before use if you have ear drainage or discharge ear pain irritation, or rash in the ear dizziness an injury or perforation (hole) of the ear drum recently had ear surgery

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:
Directions FOR USE IN THE EAR ONLY Adults and children over 12 years of age: Tilt head sideways and place 5 to 10 drops into ear Tip of applicator should not enter ear canal Keep drops in ear for several minutes by keeping head tilted or placing cotton in the ear Use twice daily for up to 4 days if needed, or as directed by a doctor Any wax remaining after treatment may be removed by gently flushing the ear with warm water, using a soft rubber bulb ear syringe Children under 12 years: consult a doctor

STOP USE IF:
Stop use and ask a doctor if you need to use for more than 4 days excessive earwax remains after use of this product
