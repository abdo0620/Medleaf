DRUG NAME: Antispetic
GENERIC NAME: CETYLPYRIDINIUM CHLORIDE
MANUFACTURER: Demoulas Super Markets, Inc
ROUTE: ORAL

ACTIVE INGREDIENT:
Active ingredient Cetylpyridinium chloride 0.07%

INACTIVE INGREDIENTS:
Inactive ingredients water, glycerin, flavor, poloxamer 188, sodium saccharin, propylene glycol, sodium benzoate, sucralose, benzoic acid, blue 1

INDICATIONS AND USAGE:
Use helps control plaque that leads to gingivitis

WARNINGS:
Warnings for this product

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:
Directions adults and children 6 years & older: rinse for 30 seconds with 20 mL (4 teaspoonfuls) twice a day do not swallow children 6 years to under 12 years of age: supervise use when used after brushing, rinse mouth with water first children under 6 years of age: do not use

ADVERSE REACTIONS:
ADVERSE REACTION DISTRIBUTED BY DEMOULAS SUPER MARKETS, INC. 875 EAST STREET TEWKSBURY, MA 01876

STOP USE IF:
Stop use and ask a dentist if gingivitis, gleeding or redness persists for more than 2 weeks you have painful or swollen gums, pus from the gum line, loose teeth or increased spacing between the teeth. These may be signs or symptoms of periodontitis, a serious form of gum disease.
