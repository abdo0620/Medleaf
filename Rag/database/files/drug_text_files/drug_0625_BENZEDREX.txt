DRUG NAME: BENZEDREX
GENERIC NAME: PROPYLHEXEDRINE
MANUFACTURER: BF ASCHER AND CO INC
ROUTE: NASAL

ACTIVE INGREDIENT:
Active ingredient................. Purpose Propylhexedrine 175 mg......Nasal decongestant

INACTIVE INGREDIENTS:
Inactive ingredients lavender oil, menthol

INDICATIONS AND USAGE:
Uses For the temporarily relief of nasal congestion due to a cold, hay fever, or other upper respiratory allergies (allergic rhinitis).

WARNINGS:
Warnings Do not exceed recommended dosage. This product may cause temporary discomfort such as burning, stinging, sneezing, or an increase in nasal discharge. The use of this container by more than one person may spread infection. Use only as directed. Frequent or prolonged use may cause nasal congestion to recur or worsen. Ill effects may result if taken internally

DO NOT USE:
Do not use this product for more than three days.

ASK A DOCTOR BEFORE USE:
Stop use and consult a doctor if symptoms persist.

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:
Directions adults and children 6 to 12 years of age (with adult supervision): two inhalations in each nostril not more than every two hours. children under 6 years of age: consult a doctor

STORAGE AND HANDLING:
Other information store at 59°-86° F (15°-30° C) keep inhaler tightly closed mfd. in USA for B.F. Ascher & Co., Inc. this inhaler is effective for a minimum of 3 months after first use
