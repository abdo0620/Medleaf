DRUG NAME: Re-Lieved Lidocaine Patch
GENERIC NAME: LIDOCAINE PATCH 4%
MANUFACTURER: Transfer Technology
ROUTE: TOPICAL

ACTIVE INGREDIENT:
Active Ingredients Lidocaine 4%

INACTIVE INGREDIENTS:
Inactive Ingredients Acrylic adhesive, Polyethylene Foam

INDICATIONS AND USAGE:
Uses For the temporary relief of pain

WARNINGS:
Warnings For external use only. The adhesoin properties of this patch may cause an extended period of redness. Please visit www.re-lieved.com for more information.

DO NOT USE:
Do not use If you are allergic to Lidocaine. On open wounds or on damaged, broken or irritated skin. Remove immidiately if you sense a burning sensation after application.

ASK A DOCTOR BEFORE USE:
Ask your doctor or pharmacist before use if you are using blood thinning medication, steroids, or non-steroidal anti-inflammatory druns (NSAIDs), or if you have sensitive skin.

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:
Directions adults/children Clean and dry affected area Peel patch off plastic liner and place patch on affected area for no more than 12 hours Wash hands after applying patch Under age 12, consult a physician before use For easier removal of the patch, apply rubbing alcohol or baby oil to adhesive edge while gently peeling. Store new and used patch away from children and pets.

STOP USE IF:
Stop use or ask a doctor if Condition worsens Symptoms persist for more than 7 days
