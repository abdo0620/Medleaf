Inactive ingredients Alcohol, Aqua, Menthyl Lactate, Borneol, Butylene Glycol, Glycerin

INDICATIONS AND USAGE:
Do not begin use of any hydrocortisone product unless you've asked a doctor. Do not use in the genital area if you have a vaginal discharge. Consult a doctor. Do not use more than directed unless told to do so by a doctor.

WARNINGS:
Warnings For external use only.

DO NOT USE:
Do not use in or near the eyes for disper rash; ask a doctor

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:
Uses Temporarily relieves itching associated with minor skin irritations, inflammation and rashes due to: eczema, psoriasis, insect bites, detergents, jewelry, cosmetics, soaps, seborrheic dermatitis and more.

STOP USE IF:
Stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens or if symptoms persist for more than 7 days symptoms last more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days

Other information Protect from excessive heat (40 o C/ 104 o F)
