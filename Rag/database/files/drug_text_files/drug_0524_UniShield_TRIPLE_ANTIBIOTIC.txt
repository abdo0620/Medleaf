DRUG NAME: UniShield TRIPLE ANTIBIOTIC
GENERIC NAME: BACITRACIN ZINC, NEOMYCIN SULFATE, POLYMYXIN B SULFATE
MANUFACTURER: Unishield
ROUTE: TOPICAL

ACTIVE INGREDIENT:
Active Ingredients (in each gram) Bacitracin zinc (bacitracin 400 units) Neomycin sulfate（neomycin 3.5 mg） Polymyxin B sulfate (polymyxin B 5,000 units)

INACTIVE INGREDIENTS:
Inactive Ingredients Mineral oil, petrolatum, purified water

INDICATIONS AND USAGE:
Uses First aid to help prevent infection in minor cuts, scrapes and burns

WARNINGS:
Warnings For external use only.

DO NOT USE:
Do not use in the eyes over large areas of the body if you are allergic to any of the ingredients longer than 1 week unless directed by a doctor.

ASK A DOCTOR BEFORE USE:
Ask a doctor before use if you have deep or puncture wounds, animal bites, or serious burns

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:
Directions Clean and disinfect the affected area Apply a small amount of this product (an amount equal to the surface area of the tip of a finger) on the area 1 to 3 times daily May be covered with a sterile bandage.

STOP USE IF:
Stop use and ask a doctor if if the condition persists or gets worse a rash or other allergic reaction develops.

STORAGE AND HANDLING:
Other Information Store at room temperature Tamper evident. Do not use if packet is torn out or opened.
