ROUTE: TOPICAL

ACTIVE INGREDIENT:
Active Ingredient Benzalkonium Chloride 0.13%

INACTIVE INGREDIENTS:
Inactive Ingredients Water (Aqua), Lauramidopropylamine Oxide, Glycerin, Cetrimonium Chloride, Sodium Chloride, Cocamide MEA, PEG-120 Methyl Glucose Dioleate, Fragrance (Parfum), Citric Acid, Tetrasodium EDTA, Sodium Sulfate, Methylchloroisothiazolinone, Methylisothiazolinone, Blue 1 (CI42090), Yellow 5 (CI 19140)

INDICATIONS AND USAGE:
Uses Helps eliminate bacteria on hands.

WARNINGS:
Warnings For external use only. When using this product Avoid contact with eyes. In case of Contact, Flush with Water. Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation or redness develops and lasts. Keep out of reach of children. In case of accidental ingestion, seek medical attention or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:
Directions From pump bottle, apply onto wet hands. Lather and rinse thoroughly.

STOP USE IF:
Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation or redness develops and lasts.

STORAGE AND HANDLING:
Other Information Store at Room Temperature.
