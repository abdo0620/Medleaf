DRUG NAME: Triple Antibiotic
GENERIC NAME: BACITRACIN ZINC, NEOMYCIN SULFATE , POLYMYXIN B SULFATE.
MANUFACTURER: TRIFECTA PHARMACEUTICALS USA LLC
ROUTE: TOPICAL

ACTIVE INGREDIENT:
Active ingredients (each gram contains) Bacitracin zinc 400 units Neomycin sulfate 3.5 mg Polymyxin B sulfate 5,000 units

INACTIVE INGREDIENTS:
Inactive ingredient light Mineral Oil, White Petrolatum

INDICATIONS AND USAGE:
Uses first aid to help prevent infection in minor:● cuts ● scrapes ● burns

WARNINGS:
Warnings For external use only.

DO NOT USE:
Do Not Use In the eyes Over large areas of the body If you are allergic to any of the ingredients

ASK A DOCTOR BEFORE USE:
Ask Doctor Before Use Ask Doctor before use if you have deep or puncture wounds, animal bites, or serious burns.

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:
Directions ● clean the affected area and dry thoroughly. ● apply a small amount of this product (an amount equal to the surface area of the tip of a finger) on the area 1 to 3 times daily ● may be covered with a sterile bandage.

STOP USE IF:
Stop Use and ask a doctor Stop Use and ask a doctor if Condition persists or gets worse You need to use longer than 1 week A rash or other allergic reaction develops
