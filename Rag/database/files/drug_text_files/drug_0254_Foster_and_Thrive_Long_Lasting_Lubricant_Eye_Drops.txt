INDICATIONS AND USAGE:
Use for the temporary relief of burning and irritation due to dryness if the eye

WARNINGS:
Warnings For external use only Do not use if this product changes color or becomes cloudy if you are sensitive to any ingredient in this product When using this product do not touch tip of container to any surface to avoid contamination replace cap after each use Stop use and ask a doctor if you feel eye pain changes in vision occur redness or irritation of the eye(s) gets worse or lasts more than 72 hours If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health professional before use. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center (1-800-222-1222) right away.

DO NOT USE:
Do not use if this product changes color or becomes cloudy if you are sensitive to any ingredient in this product

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:
Directions instill 1 or 2 drops in the affected eye(s) as needed

STOP USE IF:
Stop use and ask a doctor if you feel eye pain changes in vision occur redness or irritation of the eye(s) gets worse or lasts more than 72 hours
