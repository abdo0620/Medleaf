DRUG NAME: Equate Honey Lemon Cough Drops
GENERIC NAME: MENTHOL
MANUFACTURER: WalMart
ROUTE: ORAL

ACTIVE INGREDIENT:
Active ingredient (in each drop) Menthol 7.5 mg

INACTIVE INGREDIENTS:
Inactive ingredients: Beta carotene, corn starch, corn syrup, eucalyptus oil, glycerin, honey, medium chain triglycerides, natural and artificial flavors, soybean oil, sucrose and water.

INDICATIONS AND USAGE:
Uses temporarily relieves: cough as may occur with a cold occasional minor irritation and sore throat

WARNINGS:
Warnings Sore throat warning: if sor throat is severe, persists for more than 2 days, is accompanied or followed by fever, headache, rash, swelling, nausea, or vomiting, consult a doctor promptly. If sore mouth symptoms do not improve in 7 days, see your dentist or doctor promptly. These symptoms may be serious.

ASK A DOCTOR BEFORE USE:
Ask a doctor before use if you have persistent or chronic cough such as occurs with smoking, asthma, or emphysema cough accompanied by excessive phlegm (mucus)

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:
Directions adults and children 5 years and over - dissolve 1 lozenge slowly in mouth. Repeat every 2 hours as needed or as directed by a doctor. children under 5 years - ask a doctor

STOP USE IF:
Stop use and ask doctor if cough persists for more than 7 days, tends to recur, or is accompanied by fever, rash, or persistant headache. These could be signs of a serious condition. sore throat is severe, or irritation, pain or redness lasts or worsens sore mouth does not improve in 7 days
